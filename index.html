The Story

From Robotazia to the Future

The Original Collection

Robotazia began as an ambitious idea: to create a world where robots, imagination, recycled materials and engineering could come together in one extraordinary experience.

The collection has already been exhibited publicly, giving visitors the opportunity to see large-scale robotic creations at close range.

Today, the original collection continues to exist as a substantial body of work, with the potential to become an exhibition, educational resource, travelling collection or permanent museum attraction.

For Museums & Science Centres

Robotazia: A World Ready to Move. Is It Moving to You?

Robotazia is a growing collection of giant, hand-built robots — each one a fusion of engineering, storytelling, and reclaimed materials. Born from a passion for sci-fi and futuristic innovation, and proven with a landmark run across the UK, the collection is now being presented to museums, science centers, education organizations, and exhibition partners worldwide.

What makes Robotazia different isn't just scale — it's what these machines are made of and what they teach. Every robot is a working case study in sustainability, mechanical engineering, and creative reuse: circuits and scrap transformed into towering, larger-than-life characters that visitors don't just look at, but remember.

The collection sits at the intersection of: Large-scale visual impact and immersive design Engineering and mechanical craftsmanship Art, storytelling, and creative recycling STEM and STEAM education — brought to life, not simulated

In a moment when classrooms are racing toward AI tutors and screen-based learning, Robotazia offers something the research says kids need first: tactile, physical, hands-on discovery. There's no substitute for standing next to a robot built from real machines, real materials, real engineering — it's computational thinking, sustainability, and creativity you can walk around and touch.

We welcome conversations about exhibitions, educational programs, museum partnerships, and future opportunities.
